# Privacy Policy — SLIP REVIEW BOT

Last updated: November 18, 2026

## 1. Information Collected
The bot may receive image attachments and basic Discord interaction information required to operate.

## 2. Image Processing
Submitted images may be processed temporarily for review and privacy protection. Temporary files should be deleted after processing.

## 3. Sensitive Information
Users should avoid sharing passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary personal and financial information.

## 4. Data Sharing
The bot does not intentionally sell submitted images or personal information. Images should not be shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

## 5. Data Retention
Images are intended to be retained only as long as necessary for processing. The bot administrator is responsible for configuring storage and deletion practices.

## 6. Security
Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect uploaded files and bot credentials.

## 7. Contact
For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the bot administrator through the Discord server where the bot is deployed.
